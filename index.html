Background

I am a passionate and versatile Data Analyst with over a decade of experience bridging the worlds of accounting, HR, and education. My journey began in finance, where I developed a sharp analytical mindset managing over 200 cooperative member accounts, optimizing financial reporting, and driving data-backed decision-making. This foundation evolved into a deep enthusiasm for uncovering insights through data, solving complex problems, and influencing strategic outcomes.

Proficient in tools like Microsoft Excel, Tableau, Power BI, SQL, and Python (Pandas, NumPy), I thrive at the intersection of data analytics and business strategy. My expertise spans predictive modeling, data visualization, and process automation, allowing me to deliver actionable insights that enhance operational efficiency and organizational performance.

Highlights of my journey include:

  • Streamlining HR analytics at NNPC Medical Services Limited, improving workforce planning and retention strategies.
  • Driving financial analytics at cooperative societies, optimizing loan processes, and improving member satisfaction.
  • Implementing data-driven solutions in education, including student performance tracking and curriculum optimization.


    • My academic foundation includes a Postgraduate Diploma in Computer Science and a Bachelor’s in Accounting. I have continually upskilled through certifications like IBM’s Python for Data Science, EntryLevel’s SQL Data Analysis, and FreeCodeCamp’s Responsive Web Design, showcasing my commitment to staying ahead in the data analytics landscape.

    From creating predictive financial models to building responsive web portfolios, I bring a unique blend of technical proficiency and interpersonal skills honed through years of collaboration with diverse stakeholders. Whether it’s driving business intelligence, streamlining processes, or storytelling through data, I am dedicated to creating value and fostering data-driven cultures.

    Beyond my work, I enjoy cycling, exploring the outdoors, and embracing new adventures that spark creativity and inspiration.
