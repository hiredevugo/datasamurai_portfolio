Background

I am the Co-Founder and CEO of NaetoSoft, where I lead a dynamic team focused on harnessing the power of data and technology to create transformative software solutions. With over a decade of experience in data analytics and business strategy, my journey spans across diverse industries including accounting, HR, education, and web development. I am driven by a passion for solving complex problems and unlocking insights that lead to impactful, data-driven decisions.

My career began in finance, where I sharpened my analytical skills by managing accounts, optimizing financial reporting, and driving decision-making through data. This foundation paved the way for a deeper interest in data analytics, which eventually led me to expand my expertise into software solutions and web development.

As a data analyst and tech leader, I am proficient in tools such as Microsoft Excel, Tableau, Power BI, SQL, and Python (Pandas, NumPy). I specialize in predictive modeling, data visualization, process automation, and web development, helping organizations improve their operational efficiency, enhance user experiences, and make data-driven decisions that fuel growth. Key highlights of my career include:

  • Leading the development of data-driven solutions that optimize business processes and enhance organizational performance.
  • Designing and implementing web development projects that align with strategic goals and improve user engagement.
  • Delivering actionable insights through data analytics, predictive modeling, and process automation that have driven measurable improvements in various sectors.

    • I hold a Postgraduate Diploma in Computer Science and a Bachelor's in Accounting. In addition to my formal education, I have earned certifications like IBM’s Python for Data Science, EntryLevel’s SQL Data Analysis, and FreeCodeCamp’s Responsive Web Design, reflecting my commitment to continuous learning and staying ahead in the rapidly evolving tech landscape.

    As a leader at NaetoSoft, I am dedicated to fostering a culture of innovation, collaboration, and data-driven decision-making. I believe in the transformative power of technology to solve problems, drive business growth, and create value for organizations and individuals alike.

    Outside of work, I enjoy cycling, exploring the outdoors, and engaging in new adventures that fuel creativity and inspire fresh perspectives.
